
# Scarface (1983)

## About the Movie
Scarface is a crime drama movie released in 1983. It was directed by Brian De Palma and stars Al Pacino as Tony Montana.

## Story
The movie is about Tony Montana, a Cuban immigrant who moves to Miami and becomes a powerful drug dealer. His ambition and greed lead him to success but eventually cause his downfall.

## Main Characters
- Tony Montana
- Manny Ribera
- Elvira Hancock
- Frank Lopez

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is Tony Montana's journey to becoming successful and powerful. I like this movie because it has an interesting story and great action scenes.
